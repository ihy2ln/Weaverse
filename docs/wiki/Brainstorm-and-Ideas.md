# Brainstorm and Ideas

Brainstorm is a creative workshop with three sections: **Brainstorm** chat,
**Ideas**, and the existing **Notes** board. Chats and saved ideas are available
across the app. An idea can optionally link to a book or campaign.

## Explore alternatives

Write a rough concept in Brainstorm and tap **Explore alternatives**. The AI
returns three different directions with a premise, strengths, risks, and a
next step. Select two or more cards and tap **Combine selected**. **Develop**
expands a concept; **Challenge** looks for weak points and repairs. The same
actions are available on individual chat messages.

Alternatives remain in their chat until you tap **Save to Ideas**. A regular
message can also be saved as an idea. If a model does not return comparison
cards, its response remains readable chat text.

## Organize ideas

Saved cards start in **Inbox**. Move them to **Exploring**, **Keep**, or
**Archived** as they develop. You can edit, search, pin, and link a card to a
project. **Source** returns to the originating chat. On wide screens the board
appears beside chat; on phones open the **Ideas** section.

## Send an idea onward

**Send to…** copies a card into the shared Notes board or a chosen Codex
category. For Novel Plan, choose either a new draft scene outline in a
chapter or a beat in an existing scene. The idea card stays on the board.

## Conversation and media

Brainstorm sends recent chat turns and selected Codex context to the chosen
model. The **+** control stages up to four images for the next message; a
vision model is required for the AI to see them. The chat rail can be searched,
and its **⋯** menu renames, pins, or deletes a chat.
