# Novel and Reader

Novel opens at Bookshelf. Create a novel or select its cover card, then use the
Novel workspace menu.

## Plan and Write

Plan uses **Book → Chapter → Scene → Scene beat**. Write edits the chosen scene
with prose, beats, Codex mentions, media blocks, speech input, and the compact
AI/manual prompt dock. Long-press selected text for editing actions such as
adding it to the Codex.

## Reading

The reading view includes previous/next page and chapter controls plus jump to
top and bottom. Page changes normally scroll to the top. Turn on the
keep-position format option when retaining the vertical position is preferable.

Media added in Write belongs to the manuscript and is expected in reading and
review views. If it is missing, verify that the source asset still exists, check
Pictures, and make a backup before attempting a re-import.

## Novel card menu

Press and hold a novel for export, delete, copy, cover-art, and selection
actions. Quick remove supports multi-selection but remains destructive after
confirmation, so export anything that may be useful later.
