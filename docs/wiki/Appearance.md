# Appearance

Settings → **Appearance**. Every choice here applies to every page — Home,
Books, the manga hub, editors, dialogs and menus — the moment you make it.
A live preview at the top of the section shows the result.

## Profiles

A profile is a whole look: palette, lettering and corner shape together.
**WeaverVerse** (cinematic black, the default), **Classic**, **Fantasy**,
**Arcade**, **Synthwave**, **Chill**, **Tabletop**, **Noir**, **Storybook**,
**Cyberdeck**, **Meadow**, **Ember**, **Deep Sea**, **Sakura**, **Sunset**,
**Frost** and **Royal**. Each card in the picker is a live miniature of its
profile — its own art, glass panel, typeface and corners.

## Theme

Classic keeps all four moods (Light, Sepia, Dark, Black). The styled
profiles carry their own mood and offer light or dark. WeaverVerse is
always dark.

## Wallpaper

Drawn behind every page, Home included:

| Group | Wallpapers |
| --- | --- |
| Weaverse | None, Theme art (matches the profile) |
| Windows style | Bloom, Bloom Dark, Glow, Bliss |
| Apple style | Big Sur, Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma, Sequoia |

They are drawn by the app, not bundled images, so they cost nothing in app
size and stay sharp on any screen. **Your own wallpaper** (an image, or a
video that loops muted) replaces them.

## Glass

**Solid ↔ Clear.** Sets how much wallpaper shows through panels, cards, the
top bar and each page.

## Font size

**Small · Default · Large · Largest**, or any size from 80% to 140% on the
slider. Scales every page, menu and dialog, on top of Android's own font
size setting.

## Line height

**Compact · Default · Relaxed · Airy**, or 0.85× to 1.5× on the slider —
the space between lines of text everywhere in the app.

## Brightness

Dims the whole app, independent of section colors.

## Reader text

The book reader keeps its own text size and line height so reading can be
set larger than the rest of the app. Change them here or from the reader's
**A− / A+** controls.

## Section colors

Per-section tints layered on top of any profile. **Reset section colors**
undoes them.
