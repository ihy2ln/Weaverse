# Codex

Codex is Weaverse’s world bible (characters, places, lore).

## Mentions
- Names/aliases in prose can become tappable links when tracking is on.
- Tap → peek sheet (aliases + body excerpt).
- **Open Codex** jumps to the full entry (labeled **Codex**).

## AI inclusion
- Always-include entries are candidates every generation.
- Mention tracking adds entries when the scan text matches.
- Manual include/exclude sets exist for advanced flows.
- Token budget may drop lower-priority entries from the packed prompt.
