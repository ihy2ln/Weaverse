# Navigation and Shelves

The top chrome contains a mode menu, the current mode's workspace menu, and an
Extra menu for shared tools. Tapping a main mode returns to its home: Bookshelf,
Campaign, Chats, Window, or Notes.

Press and hold menu items to enter movement mode, then drag them into a preferred
order. The same interaction works for visible menu buttons and drop-down items.

## Shelf actions

Bookshelf, Campaign, and Window show one card per work. Tap to open; press and
hold for its context menu. The Novel menu includes export, delete, copy, cover
art, and quick-selection actions.

For multiple removals:

1. Hold an item.
2. Choose **Select for quick remove**.
3. Select more items.
4. Tap **Quick remove** and review the confirmation.

The Home screen does not duplicate create buttons, Books/Series toggles, or a
“continue where you left off” feed. Current works live under their mode.
